About Me

I am a B.Sc. graduate in Agricultural Economics and currently pursuing my MS degree in Rural Development at Gazipur Agricultural University (GAU). My academic journey combines social science research, quantitative data collection, and campus technology leadership.

Through my academic research internship at the Bangladesh Rice Research Institute (BRRI) and leadership roles in student organizations, I have developed a strong foundation in organizing data, managing youth initiatives, and making complex tools approachable for peers.

As an emerging facilitator, I don't position myself as an all-knowing guru. Instead, I am a continuous learner eager to share practical skill-sets with fellow students, campus groups, and community programs through structured learning sessions.
